
## 2024-06-25 - IDOR in Task Updates and Checklist Management
**Vulnerability:** IDOR (Insecure Direct Object Reference) vulnerabilities were present in `api/operations/tasks/[taskId]/route.ts` and `api/my-tasks/[id]/checklist/[itemId]/route.ts`. The endpoints utilized Prisma's `findFirstOrThrow` and `findUniqueOrThrow` supplying only the primary key (`taskId` or `itemId`).
**Learning:** Nested REST resources require ensuring proper relationship bounds are mapped, preventing cross-tenant access. Omitting explicit checks for `company_id` on top-level records or `task: { assigned_to }` on related nested records allows horizontal and vertical privilege escalation via manipulation of sequential IDs. Unhandled exceptions from `*OrThrow` queries may also leak HTTP 500 errors instead of returning standard 404 responses.
**Prevention:** Always scope database queries utilizing `findFirst` to combine the primary key with necessary authorization filters such as `company_id` for operations and both `company_id` and `assigned_to` for personal tasks. Respond gracefully with a 404 error instead of utilizing `findFirstOrThrow` to prevent unhandled 500 crashes and enforce strict tenant isolation across nested objects.
