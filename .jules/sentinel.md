## 2024-05-23 - Prevent IDOR in Nested Resource Operations
**Vulnerability:** Insecure Direct Object References (IDOR) were present in the task status and task checklist item status update endpoints. By directly referencing `taskId` and `itemId` without verifying the `company_id` and `assigned_to` context of the current employee, any logged-in user could manipulate the operations of tasks belonging to different employees or companies.
**Learning:** For nested resources and operational states like task statuses, it is insufficient to solely rely on the route parameters (`taskId` or `itemId`). We must verify the entity's hierarchical ownership back up to the tenant/company boundary before performing the update.
**Prevention:** Enforce relationship and ownership boundaries in Prisma mutations. Use `findFirstOrThrow` to check that the nested entity (e.g., `TaskChecklistItem`) maps to the parent entity (`task_id`), and that the parent entity belongs to the requesting employee's tenant and is assigned to them (`company_id`, `assigned_to`).
